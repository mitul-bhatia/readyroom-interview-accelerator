# Demo video plan (about 3 minutes)

Use Chrome on the deployed HTTPS site. Make sure the top-right badge says **Groq connected**, and check microphone permission before recording. Use the **Fill example documents** button; the documents are fictional, while the AI responses are live.

| Time | Show | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Setup and example documents | “Candidates often have a JD and resume but little clarity on what to prepare. Readyroom starts with both.” |
| 0:20–0:50 | Analyse; role tab; candidate fit tab | “The app extracts requirements and shows which required skills have applied evidence, partial evidence, or no evidence.” |
| 0:50–1:20 | Start screening; play question; record and transcribe one answer | “The interviewer asks about a project from this resume. I can answer by voice, inspect the transcript, and submit.” |
| 1:20–2:05 | Show follow-up and move through competency and deep-dive | “Each next question is generated after evaluating the previous answer and can probe a weak claim or increase the difficulty.” Use concise answers for the remaining five turns. |
| 2:05–2:45 | Report, competency scores, preparation plan, question feedback | “The score blends interview performance with evidence from the JD. Every answer has specific feedback and the plan ranks the next things to prepare.” |
| 2:45–3:00 | Save as PDF; close | “The goal is to leave with a clearer picture of real interview readiness.” |

## Short answers for the example run

Use your own words. These prompts keep the six-question demo moving while still giving the AI enough content to evaluate:

1. “CampusPath helped students understand job requirements. I built the React interface and integrated the API. We piloted it with 120 students and collected usefulness feedback.”
2. “We used a short survey after the pilot. I would improve the evaluation with a baseline task, a larger sample, and a clearer definition of usefulness.”
3. “I validate the response shape, show a recoverable error, and retry only for transient failures. I log the failure category without storing a student's resume.”
4. “We removed redundant onboarding steps after observing where users dropped off. I would compare completion rate and task success before and after.”
5. “The 18% claim came from a small internal test. I would specify the test set, relevance metric, and baseline before treating it as a strong result.”
6. “At ten times the usage, I would add monitoring, rate limits, a held-out evaluation set, and human review for failures. I would watch latency, cost, and answer quality.”

The AI's questions vary, so answer the question actually shown. Do not read an answer that no longer fits the conversation.

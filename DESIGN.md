# Readyroom design

An interview preparation workspace with the calm structure of an editorial brief and the focus of a recording studio. The setup and analysis screens use warm paper, dark ink, and precise blue accents. The interview screen changes to a dark, low-distraction surface. Large type is reserved for the active task; evidence, scores, and next actions receive clear labels and enough room to scan.

The visual hierarchy follows the candidate's journey: source documents, analysis, conversation, then report. One primary action per stage. Status and provenance are always visible, especially when the app is running a simulated sample session.
